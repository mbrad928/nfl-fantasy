Snake Draft Fantasy League

Four owners snake-draft the NFL, one team at a time, until all 32 are claimed — 8 teams apiece. Every regular-season win is a point, every team that makes the playoffs is worth 5 more, and a Super Bowl win is worth a flat 5. Most points wins the league.

Draft

A live snake draft over individual teams — 4 owners, 32 NFL teams, 8 rounds. Round 1 goes in the order below; each following round snakes back in reverse. How scoring works

  1. Each owner snake-drafts 8 individual NFL teams, one at a time, before the season starts.
  2. +1 point for every regular-season win across all 8 of your teams.
  3. +5 points for each of your teams that makes the playoffs.
  4. +5 points, once, if any of your teams wins the Super Bowl.
  5. Win totals refresh live from ESPN; playoff berths and the Super Bowl winner are checked off by any owner and sync to everyone in real time.

The draft, win totals, playoff berths, and the Super Bowl winner all sync live for everyone via Firestore — see README.md for one-time setup.
